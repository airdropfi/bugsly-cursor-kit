# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the `bugsly` server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. The first five only read.

1. `Which Bugsly projects do I have?`
2. `Show the 5 newest unresolved errors in my <project> Bugsly project.`
3. `Open the TypeError issue in <project>, show its stack trace and explain the likely cause. Don't change anything.`
4. `Are my Bugsly cron jobs and uptime monitors healthy, and what is <project>'s crash-free rate over 30 days?`
5. `How many events have I used this month and what is my limit?`
6. `I fixed issue <id> (<title>) in this repo. Mark that one issue as resolved.` (changes the status for everyone on the project)
